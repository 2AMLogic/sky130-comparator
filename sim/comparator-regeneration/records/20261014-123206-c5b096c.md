Supply-headroom finding for CLAUDE.md's 1.8 V constraint: at -10 % supply the latch's small-signal regeneration does not start from a 1 mV input.
2. **ss/-40C/1.8 V**: 1 mV partly (0.64 / 0.35) and 0.1 mV not at all (0.50). Centring (gf180's `dut_vos` probe) is not implemented; candidate follow-up for #170.
3. The polarity thresholds are 0.75/0.25 not gf180's 0.9/0.1: the losing output stops short of the rail at the small rungs (0.88 at tt/27C/1.8 V), see DR-008.
